AI Student Attendance, Engagement & Intervention Agent
Domain: College / Education / Predictive AI / Agentic AI
Problem Statement: Attendance alone does not provide a complete view of student engagement. Faculty
and mentors need to combine attendance, assessments, assignments, LMS activity and other authorized
indicators.
Objective: Develop an AI agent that analyzes academic-engagement signals, detects meaningful changes,
explains contributing factors and recommends personalized interventions to faculty mentors.
Expected Prototype Features
● Attendance, assessment, assignment and LMS integration.
● Engagement trend and anomaly detection.
● Explainable academic-risk profiles.
● Personalized intervention recommendations.
● Faculty mentor prioritization dashboard.
● Automated mentor summaries and action lists.
● Intervention tracking and outcome monitoring.
● Role-based access and privacy controls.
